Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (which is encrypted before storage — we never store or see it in plain text). When you use Chat, your messages are sent to our AI provider to generate a response and are saved to your account so your conversation history is there next time you log in.

How your data is stored

Account details and conversation history are stored securely and tied to your account. They are not sold or shared with advertisers.

Payments

Payments are processed directly by PayPal. We do not receive or store your card or bank details — PayPal handles that under its own privacy and security terms.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated conversation history at any time by contacting support. Clearing your browser's site data will also remove locally cached information on that device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@vampai.example.

This policy is provided for a prototype product and reflects its actual current practices. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and should be before handling real customer data at scale.
